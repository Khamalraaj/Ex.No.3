## Aim

To create and evaluate prompts using four different prompt engineering techniques: Straightforward Prompting, Tabular Format Prompting, Preceding Question Prompting, and Missing Word Prompting, and to generate a report using one selected use case from Unit 5.

## Explanation

Selected Use Case: AI in Healthcare – Early Disease Detection and Diagnosis

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly used in healthcare to support doctors in identifying diseases, analyzing medical images, predicting health risks, and improving patient care. This use case is selected to demonstrate how different prompting techniques can generate information in different formats.

The four prompting techniques are applied to the topic of AI in healthcare. Their outputs are compared based on clarity, accuracy, organization, completeness, and ease of understanding.

## Procedure

### 1. Straightforward Prompts

Straightforward prompting gives direct and clear instructions to an AI model without providing additional examples or complex conditions.

Example Prompts:
```
Explain how Artificial Intelligence helps in early
disease detection.

Include:
1. Definition of AI in healthcare
2. Five applications
3. Three benefits
4. Real-world examples

Use simple student-friendly language.
```

Generated Answer:

Artificial Intelligence in healthcare refers to the use of computer systems and machine learning techniques to support medical professionals in diagnosing diseases, analyzing patient information, and making healthcare decisions.

Five applications of AI in medical diagnosis are:

1. Medical Image Analysis: AI analyzes X-rays, CT scans, and MRI images to identify possible abnormalities.
2. Disease Prediction: AI examines patient information to estimate the risk of developing certain diseases.
3. Cancer Detection: AI assists in identifying suspicious patterns in medical images that may indicate cancer.
4. Patient Monitoring: AI-powered systems analyze health data and identify unusual changes that may require medical attention.
5. Clinical Decision Support: AI provides relevant information to help doctors evaluate possible diagnoses and treatment options.

Benefits of AI in healthcare:

- Helps identify possible diseases earlier.

- Reduces the time required to analyze large amounts of medical data.

- Supports doctors in making informed decisions.

- Improves the efficiency of healthcare services.

AI supports medical professionals but does not replace the need for qualified doctors or clinical judgment.

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting

Tabular format prompting instructs an AI model to present information in rows and columns. It is useful for comparing concepts and organizing information clearly.

Example Prompts:

1. "Compare five applications of AI in healthcare, their functions, and their benefits in a table."

2. "Prepare a table showing the differences between traditional disease diagnosis and AI-assisted diagnosis."

3. "List AI technologies used in healthcare, their purposes, and examples in tabular format."

Generated Answer:

Table 1: Applications of AI in Healthcare

| AI Application            | Function                             | Benefit                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Medical Image Analysis    | Examines medical scans               | Helps identify abnormalities       |
| Disease Prediction        | Analyzes patient data                | Supports early risk identification |
| Cancer Detection          | Identifies suspicious image patterns | Assists doctors in screening       |
| Patient Monitoring        | Tracks health information            | Helps identify unusual changes     |
| Virtual Health Assistants | Provides basic health information    | Improves access to information     |

Table 2: Traditional Diagnosis vs AI-Assisted Diagnosis

| Traditional Diagnosis                                       | AI-Assisted Diagnosis                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Depends mainly on doctors' examination and experience       | Uses computational analysis to support doctors            |
| Medical images are reviewed manually                        | AI can identify patterns in medical images                |
| Large datasets may take considerable time to examine        | AI can process large datasets efficiently                 |
| Decisions rely on clinical expertise and available evidence | AI provides additional information for clinical decisions |
| Human judgment is central                                   | Human judgment remains essential, supported by AI         |

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

Preceding question prompting provides a question before the main instruction. This helps establish the topic and context so that the AI model can generate a more focused answer.

Example Prompts:

1. "What are the major challenges doctors face when diagnosing diseases? Explain how Artificial Intelligence can help address these challenges."

2. "Why is early disease detection important? Explain how AI technologies support early diagnosis."

3. "How can medical imaging help identify diseases? Describe how AI analyzes X-rays, CT scans, and MRI images."

Generated Answer:

Question: Why is early disease detection important, and how does Artificial Intelligence support it?

Early disease detection is important because identifying a disease at an early stage can provide patients with more opportunities for timely medical evaluation and treatment. It may also help prevent certain conditions from becoming more serious.

Artificial Intelligence supports early disease detection through several methods:

1. Medical Image Processing: AI systems analyze medical images such as X-rays and MRI scans to identify patterns that may be difficult to notice.

2. Data Analysis: AI examines medical records and test results to identify patterns associated with particular diseases.

3. Risk Prediction: Machine learning models analyze available patient information to estimate the risk of certain medical conditions.

4. Continuous Monitoring: AI-powered monitoring systems can identify unusual changes in patient health data.

5. Decision Support: AI provides doctors with additional information that can assist in evaluating possible diseases.

Therefore, AI can improve the speed and efficiency of medical analysis. However, its predictions must be reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals before making medical decisions.

### 4. Missing Word Prompting

Missing word prompting provides an incomplete sentence with one or more blanks. The AI model is instructed to fill in the missing information using its knowledge and the given context.

Example Prompts:

1. "Artificial Intelligence in healthcare helps doctors to detect diseases at an \_\_\_\_\_\_ stage."

2. "AI systems analyze medical images such as X-rays, CT scans, and \_\_\_\_\_\_."

3. "Machine learning is a branch of \_\_\_\_\_\_ that enables systems to learn from data."

4. "AI-based disease prediction systems analyze patient \_\_\_\_\_\_ to identify possible health risks."

Generated Answer:

1. Artificial Intelligence in healthcare helps doctors to detect diseases at an early stage.

2. AI systems analyze medical images such as X-rays, CT scans, and MRI scans.

3. Machine learning is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables systems to learn from data.

4. AI-based disease prediction systems analyze patient data to identify possible health risks.

## Evaluation of Prompting Techniques

The four prompting techniques are evaluated based on the clarity, accuracy, organization, completeness, and ease of understanding of their generated outputs.

| Prompting Technique          | Evaluation                                            | Best Use                           |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Straightforward Prompting    | Produces direct and easy-to-understand answers        | Definitions and short explanations |
| Tabular Format Prompting     | Organizes information systematically                  | Comparisons and structured reports |
| Preceding Question Prompting | Provides context and encourages detailed explanations | Concept-based questions            |
| Missing Word Prompting       | Tests knowledge through sentence completion           | Fill-in-the-blank exercises        |

Overall Analysis:

- Straightforward prompting is useful for obtaining quick answers.

- Tabular format prompting is suitable for comparing multiple concepts.

- Preceding question prompting is effective for generating detailed, context-based explanations.

- Missing word prompting is useful for checking knowledge and understanding of important terms.

Among these techniques, preceding question prompting is suitable for generating a detailed explanatory report, while tabular format prompting is more effective for presenting comparisons.
