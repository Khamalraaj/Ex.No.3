## Aim

To create and evaluate prompts using four different prompt engineering techniques: Straightforward Prompting, Tabular Format Prompting, Preceding Question Prompting, and Missing Word Prompting, and to generate a report using one selected use case from Unit 5.

## Explanation

Selected Use Case: AI in Healthcare – Early Disease Detection and Diagnosis

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly used in healthcare to support doctors in identifying diseases, analyzing medical images, predicting health risks, and improving patient care. This use case is selected to demonstrate how different prompting techniques can generate information in different formats.

The four prompting techniques are applied to the topic of AI in healthcare. Their outputs are compared based on clarity, accuracy, organization, completeness, and ease of understanding.

## Procedure

### 1. Straightforward Prompts

Straightforward prompting gives direct and clear instructions to an AI model without providing additional examples or complex conditions.

```
Explain how Artificial Intelligence helps in early
disease detection.

Include:
1. Definition of AI in healthcare
2. Five applications
3. Three benefits
4. Real-world examples

Use simple student-friendly language.
```

## Generated Answer:

 AI in Healthcare – Early Disease Detection

 1. Definition of AI in Healthcare

   Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare uses computer systems and machine learning to analyze medical data, identify diseases, and help doctors make             decisions. It helps detect diseases at an early stage.

 2. Five Applications

  Cancer Detection: Identifies cancer using MRI and mammogram images.
  Diabetes Detection: Analyzes blood sugar levels and retinal images.
  Heart Disease Detection: Examines ECG reports and heart rate.
  Brain Tumor Detection: Identifies abnormal growth using MRI scans.
  Pneumonia Detection: Detects possible pneumonia from chest X-rays.

 3. Three Benefits

  Early Detection: Helps identify diseases before they become severe.
  Faster Diagnosis: Analyzes medical reports quickly.
  Better Accuracy: Helps doctors identify abnormalities and make informed decisions.

 4. Real-World Examples

  Google DeepMind: Uses AI research to identify eye diseases from retinal images.
  IDx-DR: Detects diabetic retinopathy using eye scans.
  Aidoc: Uses AI to identify urgent medical conditions from CT scans.

### Conclusion

AI helps doctors detect diseases early, provide faster diagnosis, and improve patient care. It supports doctors but does not replace them.

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting

Tabular format prompting instructs an AI model to present information in rows and columns. It is useful for comparing concepts and organizing information clearly.

Example Prompts:
```
Now use Tabular Format Prompting.

Explain AI applications in healthcare in a table.

Include these columns:
1. AI Application
2. Technology Used
3. Function
4. Benefits
5. Example

Provide at least five applications.
```

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

Preceding question prompting provides a question before the main instruction. This helps establish the topic and context so that the AI model can generate a more focused answer.

Example Prompts:
```
Now use Preceding Question Prompting.

Why is early disease detection important
in healthcare?

Based on this question, explain how AI helps
doctors detect diseases at an early stage.

Include medical imaging, machine learning,
risk prediction and patient monitoring.
```

## Generated Answer:

Why is Early Disease Detection Important in Healthcare?

Early disease detection helps identify diseases before they become serious. It allows patients to receive timely treatment, reduces complications, and improves survival rates.

How AI Helps Doctors Detect Diseases Early

1\. Medical Imaging: AI analyzes X-rays, MRI, CT scans, and ultrasound images to identify abnormal patterns such as tumors and infections.

2\. Machine Learning: ML algorithms analyze patient data and medical reports to identify possible diseases.

3\. Risk Prediction: AI predicts the risk of diseases like diabetes, heart disease, and cancer using patient health information.

4\. Patient Monitoring: AI monitors heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels, and other health measurements to identify unusual changes.

Benefits

- Detects diseases at an early stage.
- Helps doctors provide faster treatment.
- Reduces serious health complications.

Conclusion: AI supports doctors by analyzing medical data quickly and identifying early warning signs, helping improve patient care.


### 4. Missing Word Prompting

Missing word prompting provides an incomplete sentence with one or more blanks. The AI model is instructed to fill in the missing information using its knowledge and the given context.

Example Prompts:

```
Now use Missing Word Prompting.

Complete the following sentences:

1. AI helps doctors detect diseases at an
   ______ stage.

2. MRI stands for ______.

3. Machine learning is a subset of ______.

4. AI analyzes medical ______ to identify
   abnormalities.

5. Deep learning uses artificial ______.

Provide correct answers with short explanations.
```
## Generated Answer:

### Missing Word Prompting – Answers

1\. AI helps doctors detect diseases at an early stage.\
Explanation: Early detection helps provide timely treatment.

2\. MRI stands for Magnetic Resonance Imaging.\
Explanation: It is used to create detailed images of body organs.

3\. Machine learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.\
Explanation: It enables computers to learn from data.

4\. AI analyzes medical images to identify abnormalities.\
Explanation: It examines X-rays, MRI and CT scans.

5\. Deep learning uses artificial neural networks.\
Explanation: These networks learn patterns from large amounts of data.

### Comparison of Four Prompting Techniques
